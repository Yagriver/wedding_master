Guest answers are separate from your private planning notes. All members of a household currently share its attendance, hotel, and bus choices. Older responses may need individual names confirmed.

How to use this together
  1. Add each invited household using the same name the lead guest will use to RSVP.
  2. Use groups for family, friends, or any categories you find useful.
  3. Check awaiting responses and unmatched registrations regularly.
  4. Confirm dietary needs and transport before sending filtered lists to suppliers.
  5. Assign tables once attendance is clearer. Keep decisions in private notes.

CSV exports contain the current filtered rows. Catering and transport exports exclude private notes. Only confirmed attendees are included in supplier exports.
